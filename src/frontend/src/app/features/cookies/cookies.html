1. Overview

{{ appName }} uses browser local storage to enhance your user experience. We do not use third-party cookies or tracking technologies. This policy explains what we store locally in your browser and why.

2. What We Store

All data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to external servers except when explicitly requested by your actions (e.g., package searches).

Key Purpose Type Duration
pkgdiff-theme Stores your theme preference (light/dark mode) Functional Persistent
pkgdiff-auth Session information (admin only) Essential Token lifetime
recentRegistries Recent registry selections for quick access Functional Persistent
pkgdiff-cookie-consent Your cookie consent choice Essential Persistent

All items marked as "Functional" or "Essential" are necessary for the Service to operate correctly. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

3. Third-Party Cookies

{{ appName }} does not integrate with any third-party services that set cookies or track users. All functionality is self-contained within our application.

4. Managing Your Data

You can delete all locally stored data at any time through your browser settings:

  • Chrome/Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data → See all site data and permissions → pkgdiff.dev → Remove
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data → pkgdiff.dev → Remove Selected
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → pkgdiff.dev → Remove

Clearing local storage will reset your theme preference and consent choice. Essential functional data (like auth tokens) will also be removed, logging you out if applicable.
