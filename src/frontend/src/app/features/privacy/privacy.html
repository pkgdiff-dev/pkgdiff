1. Overview

This policy explains what personal data {{ appName }} ("the Service", "we") processes when you visit {{ siteUrl }}, why, for how long, and which rights you have. {{ appName }} needs no account to use, sets no tracking cookies and shows no ads. We keep only what is needed to run the Service and protect it from abuse.

2. Who Is Responsible

The data controller is {{ controllerName }}, the individual developer who operates {{ appName }} (based in {{ controllerCountry }}). You can reach the controller about anything in this policy at {{ contactEmail }}.

3. Information We Process

3.1 Request Logs

For every request to our API we record your IP address, your browser's user agent string, the requested path, the HTTP method, the response status and the time. We use these logs to detect abuse, enforce rate limits and diagnose errors.

3.2 Search Data

When you compare package versions, we store the package name, the selected versions, whether prereleases were included, the time, how long the analysis took and your IP address. Package names and versions feed the public "recent" and "popular" lists; your IP address is never shown publicly and is used only to spot automated abuse.

3.3 Rate Limiting and Blocking

Short-lived counters keyed by IP address enforce rate limits and expire on their own. An IP address that attacks or overloads the Service can be blocked, temporarily or permanently, together with the reason for the block.

3.4 Analytics

We measure traffic with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, uses no browser storage and does not fingerprint or follow individual visitors. It reports aggregated data such as the page visited, the referring site, browser and device type, country and page load performance.

3.5 Administrator Accounts

There is no public sign-up. The only accounts are those of the Service's administrators, for whom we store a username, an email address and a hashed password, never the password itself.

3.6 Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you.

4. Why We Process It (Legal Basis)

  • Request logs, search data, rate limiting, blocking and analytics: our legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable and free Service and understanding how it is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 5(2)(f) of Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, "KVKK").
  • Administrator accounts and email correspondence: our legitimate interest in operating the Service and answering the people who contact us (the same provisions).

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, build profiles of you, or make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Service Providers and International Transfers

We share personal data only with the providers needed to run the Service:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — every request to {{ appName }} passes through Cloudflare for DNS, TLS, caching, DDoS protection and Web Analytics, so Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details on our behalf.
  • Our server hosting provider — the virtual server that runs the application, its database and its backups.
  • Google Fonts — the site's fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details when your browser downloads them.
  • GitHub and other content hosts — contributor avatars and images embedded in release notes are loaded by your browser directly from where they are hosted, mostly GitHub.

Cloudflare, Google and GitHub may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Cloudflare processes it under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

To build release notes, our server fetches public data from NuGet.org, npmjs.com, PyPI, pub.dev and GitHub, and security data from sources such as OSV and deps.dev. These requests come from our server and contain package names and versions only — never your IP address or any other data about you.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, the KVKK (Article 11) and similar laws, you can:

  • Ask whether we process data about you and get a copy of it
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests
  • Ask us to restrict processing, or to hand your data over in a portable format
  • Learn who we have shared it with
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), in the EU the data protection authority of your country

To use any of these rights, email {{ contactEmail }}. Most of our records are keyed only by IP address, so please include the IP address(es) and the approximate dates concerned. We answer within 30 days.
