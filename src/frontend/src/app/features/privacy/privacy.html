1. Overview

{{ appName }} is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. We designed the Service to minimize data collection — we only collect what is necessary to operate and improve {{ appName }}.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Search Data

When you search for a package, we store the package name, selected versions, and a timestamp of the search. This data is used to display recent searches and improve the Service.

2.2 Technical Data

We may collect your IP address, browser type, and general usage patterns through server logs. This information is used for security monitoring, rate limiting, and diagnosing technical issues.

2.3 Account Data

If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted password. We never store passwords in plain text.

3. What We Don't Collect

  • We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties
  • We do not collect financial or payment information
  • We do not track your browsing activity outside of {{ appName }}

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To display recent and popular package searches
  • To protect against abuse and enforce rate limits
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues
  • To improve the Service based on usage patterns

5. Data Storage & Retention

Data is stored on secured servers. Cached package data is automatically purged after 30 days and refreshed from upstream sources. Search history is retained for operational purposes and may be periodically cleaned. You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

{{ appName }} retrieves data from NuGet.org, npmjs.com, and GitHub to display package information. When you search for a package, we make requests to these services on your behalf. These services have their own privacy policies that govern how they handle data.
