1. Overview

{{ appName }} sets no cookies and uses no tracking technologies. It keeps a few preferences in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. This policy lists exactly what is stored and why.

2. What We Store

These entries live only in your browser (localStorage, or sessionStorage where noted). They are never sent to our servers or to anyone else.

Key Purpose Type Duration
pkgdiff-theme Stores your theme preference (light/dark mode) Functional Persistent
pkgdiff-auth Session information (admin only) Essential Token lifetime
lastSelectedRegistry The registry you last selected on the home page Functional Persistent
recentRegistries Recent registry selections for quick access Functional Persistent
pkgdiff-cookie-consent Remembers that you dismissed the privacy notice Essential Persistent
pkgdiff-linkedin-oauth-state Protects the LinkedIn sign-in flow (admin only, sessionStorage) Essential Until the flow completes

"Essential" entries are needed for the Service to work; "Functional" entries only remember your choices. None of them identify or track you, so they need no consent.

3. Analytics and Third Parties

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not track you across sites or visits.

No third-party service sets cookies on {{ appName }}. Your browser does contact Google Fonts and GitHub to load fonts and images; see the Privacy Policy for what they receive.

4. Managing Your Data

You can delete all locally stored data at any time through your browser settings:

  • Chrome/Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data → See all site data and permissions → pkgdiff.dev → Remove
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data → pkgdiff.dev → Remove Selected
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → pkgdiff.dev → Remove

Clearing it resets your theme and registry choices and shows the privacy notice again. Administrators are also signed out.
